Energy Map privacy policy

How the app uses your data.

Your data stays on your device

Energy Map stores your profile, food log, measurements, sessions and health readings in the app's storage on your phone. It has no account and no server-side copy of your data.

Health data (Health Connect / Apple Health)

With your permission, Energy Map reads:

  • Steps — to estimate the calories you burn walking.
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate and VO2 max — to personalise your heart-rate zones, estimate the calories burned in heart-rate sessions and their after-burn, and show your resting heart-rate trend.
  • Workouts — so you can import a workout's time and heart rate as a session.

On Android the app can also write test step data, used only by a developer debugging helper. Health data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never sent off your device.

Bluetooth and camera

Bluetooth is used only to connect to a heart-rate sensor you pair. The camera is used to scan food barcodes and, if you choose, to measure your pulse from your fingertip; frames are analysed on the device and never stored or sent.

What is sent over the internet

  • Food search text, to look up foods in the online food database.
  • Barcodes you scan, to look up the product on OpenFoodFacts.
  • Messages (and photos you attach) in the AI food chat, to the AI model that turns them into food entries.

None of these requests include your health data.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. You can revoke health, Bluetooth and camera access at any time in your phone's settings, and in Health Connect or the Health app.
